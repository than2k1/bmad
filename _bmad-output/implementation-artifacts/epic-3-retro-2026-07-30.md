## 4. Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned

1. **Non-Blocking Overlay Pointer Events:** Setting `pointerEvents="box-none"` on container SVG overlays is critical to ensure underlying camera HUD controls (shutter button, framing chips, mode switcher) remain fully interactive during pose alignment.
2. **Keypoint Coordinate Normalization:** Normalizing keypoints into $(0..1)$ canvas space relative to subject bounding boxes guarantees resolution independence across different mobile screen aspect ratios.
3. **Memoized HUD Cues & Lookups:** Memoizing pose catalog lookups and SVG connection path computations (`useMemo`) prevents redundant re-renders during high-frequency camera HUD updates.
